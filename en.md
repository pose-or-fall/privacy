# Privacy Policy — Pose or Fall

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in **Pose or Fall** (the "game"), a
mobile game for Android by TryBunnyhop ("we", "us").

## 1. Who we are

- Developer: TryBunnyhop
- Contact: contact@trybunnyhop.vip

## 2. Summary

- You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or
  any other information that identifies you.
- Your game progress and settings stay on your device. We do not run servers that receive
  them.
- The game shows ads through **Google AdMob**. Google may collect certain device
  information to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

## 3. Information we collect

**We do not collect personal information ourselves.**

The following is stored **only on your device** so the game works:

- Game progress, saves and records
- Settings (sound, language, graphics)
- Records needed for ads, such as how many rewarded ads you watched today and when

This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app
data.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads (ads you choose to watch for an in-game
reward) using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game may collect or receive:

- Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking)
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- Device and operating system information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
- App information (app ID, version)
- Ad interactions (ads shown, taps on ads)
- Diagnostic data about ad loading and performance

Google uses this information to deliver ads, personalize ads where permitted, measure ad
performance and prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive this information in a form that
identifies you.

How Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We never pay you, or give in-game rewards, for tapping ads. Rewarded ads give their reward
only for watching an ad you chose to watch.

## 5. Your choices and consent

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** on first launch the game shows Google's consent message. You
  can change your choice at any time with the **Privacy settings** button in the game's
  settings. The button appears only for players in regions where consent is required.
- **US states with privacy laws:** where applicable, you can opt out of the sale or sharing
  of personal information for targeted advertising with the same **Privacy settings** button.
- **Personalized ads on Android:** Settings › Google › Ads, where you can delete or reset
  your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
- **Personalized ads on iOS:** Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
- **Rewarded ads are optional.** You can play without ever watching one.

## 6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through
the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

## 7. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share information with anyone except
Google as described in section 4, which is needed to show ads.

## 8. International transfers

Google may process advertising information on servers outside your country, including in
the United States, under Google's own privacy safeguards.

## 9. Retention and deletion

- Data on your device: kept until you uninstall the game or clear its app data.
- Data received by Google: kept according to Google's Privacy Policy.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA, Korea's
Personal Information Protection Act, Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information
or Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete
or object to the processing of your personal information. Because we do not hold personal
information ourselves, requests about advertising data are handled through Google and the
privacy options above. You can still contact us and we will help.

## 11. Security

Game data stays on your device, and ad traffic is handled by Google's SDK using encrypted
connections.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant
changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.

## 13. Contact

TryBunnyhop — contact@trybunnyhop.vip
